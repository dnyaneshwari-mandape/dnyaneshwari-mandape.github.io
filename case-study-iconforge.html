01Context & Opportunity

Icon libraries don't scale the way products do.

Every new feature, dashboard, or platform needs icons — but design bandwidth grows slower than product surface area. Teams patch the gap with generic stock libraries (killing brand distinctiveness) or commission custom work (slow, expensive, and impossible to keep consistent past the first dozen assets).

The opportunity wasn't "AI that draws pictures." It was a systemized pipeline that treats icons as tokens — consistent, versioned, exportable at the scale a real design system demands.

05System Architecture

The core IA decision: icons as tokens, not one-off images.

Generation is the easy part. The design problem was building a pipeline that keeps 200 icons as consistent as 2 — so the product scales with a design system instead of fighting it.

13Reflection & Next

The highest-leverage decision wasn't a screen. It was Consistency Lock.

Treating generation as a systems problem — not a single magic screen — is what let this scale from "make one icon" to "maintain a hundred." That reframe, more than any visual choice, is the actual design contribution here.

Next: a Figma plugin for direct hand-off, multi-language prompt support, and per-brand versioned token sets for enterprise teams managing more than one product line.
